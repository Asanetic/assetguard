DISTURBANCE — CONSECUTIVE-STREAK COUNT (gap + clean + close reset)
=================================================================
Count = the CURRENT consecutive disturbance streak. It resets at the LATEST of:
  • a QUIET GAP: disturbance packets more than DISTURB_STREAK_GAP_MIN apart
    (default 10 min) are separate episodes — so an OLD unfinished disturbance
    (device powered off while disturbed, then woke already disturbed) does NOT
    bridge into a new one and jump straight to 4. THIS is the fix for "raised on
    the 1st instance / no early SMS/emails".
  • a CLEAN packet (bit 0) — device settled.
  • the last disturbance alarm CLOSE (close-driven reset — not calendar day).
  • an optional simulator floor.

Graduated within a streak: #1 ignore · #2,#3 SMS+email only · #4 raise the alarm
(the raise also notifies) · while open -> event until closed.
So you get messages on #2, #3, and #4 (3 sets), and the ALARM on #4.

Escalation (close-driven): Geofence after Disturbance is raised, OR a fallback
after off-site >= GEOFENCE_FALLBACK_MIN (default 5 min) with no disturbance;
Critical Motion after Geofence.

Verified: yesterday x3 + 1 today -> counts 1 (not 4); +2 -> 3; +1 -> 4 (raise);
clean packet -> 0.

TUNABLES (.env, optional):
  DISTURB_STREAK_GAP_MIN=10     # minutes; gap that starts a new disturbance episode
  GEOFENCE_FALLBACK_MIN=5       # minutes off-site before geofence fires without disturbance
