ALARM ESCALATION ORDER: DISTURBANCE -> GEOFENCE -> CRITICAL MOTION
=================================================================
Within a packet (and across the EAT day, per device), alarms now escalate in order:

  1) DISTURBANCE is handled FIRST (graduated):
        #1 ignore · #2 & #3 SMS+email only (no system alarm) · #4 raise the alarm
        · #5+/already open -> event. New EAT day resets.
  2) GEOFENCE is raised ONLY AFTER the Disturbance alarm is in the system
        (i.e. the 4th disturbance has raised it, or it's already open today).
        Until then a geofence breach is HELD.
  3) CRITICAL MOTION is raised ONLY AFTER the Geofence alarm is in the system.

So even if one packet carries geofence (and/or critical motion), disturbance goes
first; geofence waits for disturbance; critical motion waits for geofence. On the
packet where disturbance is raised, geofence (then critical motion) can raise in the
same pass if present. Battery / temperature / offline / low-data alarms are NOT part
of the chain and fire normally.

CAVEAT (by request): if a device never sends the disturbance bit (00100008), a
geofence breach stays HELD. The rule assumes disturbance "will most of the time be
there". Tell me if you want a fallback (e.g. geofence after N minutes off-site even
without a disturbance).

  python3 -c "import zipfile; zipfile.ZipFile('assetguard-alarm-escalation.zip').extractall('.')"
  npm run build
  pm2 restart assetguard-3010 gps-3000
  (No DB migration. Early-warning SMS/email are logged to notifications — apply the
   db/notifications.sql grant if you haven't, or the count/log stays 0.)
