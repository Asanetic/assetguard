       → balance parser reads `credit` correctly → shows "99.4" on the SMS provider row. ✓
  getdlr    → {"response-code":200,"message-id":"…","delivery-status":32,
               "delivery-description":"DeliveredToTerminal", …}

  wrapped or top-level shape, so the id we store matches what getdlr expects.
